Making a complex choice simpler

The existing experience packed comparison, pricing and purchase into one screen. I redesigned the journey to make the decision simpler and more focused.

Platforms
Desktop · Mobile web · App
Impact
+25% CTR  ·  +5% CVR

Page Before

Plans, discounts, add ons, pricing and CTAs were all fighting for space, making it difficult to know what to focus on first.

Compare → Decide → Pay all happening together

  • Users had to compare plans while simultaneously thinking about purchase decisions.
  • As users are ready to commit to a plan, we introduced a new decision: “Do I want AI?” If unsure about the feature, they may pause, get distracted, and abandon the purchase.
  • Discounts, add ons and FOMO competed for attention.

After Redesign

We restructured the experience into two focused stages, helping users first choose the right plan and then move seamlessly into reviewing and completing their purchase.

01Selection: Find the right plan

The first step is focused entirely on helping users understand and compare their options. We simplified the hierarchy around plan value and benefits, making it easier to see the differences and identify the plan that best fits their needs.

02Commitment: Review and complete the purchase

Once a plan is selected, the focus shifts from exploration to commitment. Users can now review their plan, choose relevant add ons, understand the final price and complete their purchase without having to navigate through the comparison experience again.

The Impact

A clearer journey, stronger results

The redesign resulted in:

  • 25% increase in CTR
  • 5% increase in CVR
